Developing a Global Sustainability Plan – One Community Weekly Progress Update #702

 Posted on August 31, 2026 by One Community Hs

 Leave a Comment

At One Community, developing a global sustainability plan starts with building working models we can freely share with the world. Our all-volunteer team creates self-replicating solutions spanning food, energy, housing, education, and economics, forming the basis of a worldwide collaboration of teacher/demonstration hubs. In pursuit of “The Highest Good of All,” we open source and freely share our complete process so it can be replicated anywhere, by anyone. It’s how we’re turning evolving sustainability into a practical, global roadmap for regenerating our planet.
