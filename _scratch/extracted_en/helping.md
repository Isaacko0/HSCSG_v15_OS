# Ways to Help

This page is about how anyone can help One Community create historic global change that will positively impact every person and living creature on our shared planet.

We are interested in working with those who agree this is possible and want to help create it for The Highest Good of All . You can click the following links to jump directly to the different options for helping on this page:

## WE’RE BUILDING OUR MANAGEMENT TEAM

The minimum weekly volunteer hours for joining One Community is 5 hours. We are also building our management team. If you feel you have the necessary experience for a management position, the minimum contribution is 20-25 weekly volunteer hours. If a management/leadership position is something you’d be interested in and have the time for, please indicate this in your application.

- A person seeking to share experience and build their business (entrepreneurs and business owners)

- A person seeking humanitarian and/or philanthropic contributions (world-change professionals)
