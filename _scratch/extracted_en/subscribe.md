# Subscribe for Weekly Updates

To subscribe to our weekly updates, just enter your email below and click “Subscribe”.

You will receive a confirmation email to make sure you are a human and actually own the email you’ve input, then a “Thank You” email once you confirm. After that, you’ll only ever receive one email a week from us. Check your spam folder and whitelist us if you don’t get these in your inbox .

If you ever want to stop receiving our weekly progress updates, just use the “unsubscribe” button that is always included at the bottom of each email.
