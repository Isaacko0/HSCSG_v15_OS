# Sustainable World Development: Projecting Our First 5 Years of Global Contribution and Transformation

One Community is facilitating the evolution of our planet and our species to sustainably live and create. Our path to accomplishing this is to inspire people with a working model . This model is designed for widespread appeal by being affordable, applicable across a diversity of cultures, easily accessible, created so that people with average knowledge and little or no experience can duplicate it, and with a marketing engine capable of exposing enough people to build the necessary momentum for it to continue on its own . We see this as having the ability to create a New Golden Age of cooperation, collaboration, innovation, creativity, sustainable living, and increased happiness for everyone and everything on this planet. *self-replicating and self-sufficient teacher/demonstration communities, villages, and cities*

With our current projected workforce, we anticipate meeting an ambitious five to ten year goal to complete the majority of One Community and demonstrate all 7 sustainable communal housing village models . As the first of what will be many collaborating teacher/demonstration communities, villages, and cities , and the world leader in open source sustainable world development tools, tutorials, and resources , we feel we will be able to successfully invite the necessary collaborative partners and other people interested in creating world change of this magnitude with us. By maintaining the productivity and strategy of our first year, the One Community model is designed to grow and expand to a 5-10 year accomplishment of:

- 300 full-time residents hosting 1,000’s of visitors , volunteers, and students

- 7 different eco-architecture village models with open source blueprints for reproduction

- Tens of thousands of step-by-step tutorials covering all aspects of comprehensive sustainability

- 1,000’s of additional videos and other tools covering all parts of the One Community way of living

- 100s of acres of food forest creation as well as open source sharing 6 different indoor growing food structures (demonstrating 5 different internal growing environments) and unparalleled food diversity through plants sourced from around the world as part of the One Community open source botanical garden model

- Creation of the necessary internet infrastructure, affiliate programs, and industry partnerships so that someone anywhere in the world will look at our sustainable housing models , food infrastructure , and energy infrastructure and use our tutorials and blueprints to not only design the combination of each of these that best suits their needs and budget, but to also “one click” order any components that need to be shipped in simple and affordable pre-designed packages and have it all delivered wherever needed in the world

### SUMMARY

One Community is about moving beyond a model solution by building a solution creating model . This means a regenerative and better long-term approach to sustainable world development because One Community is not designed as just one solution, but rather as a creator of modular sustainability components and solutions. One Community and our duplicable model and free blueprints include everything from sustainable food , sustainable energy , and sustainable and duplicable eco-housing , to recreation, education , fulfilled living practices , and social architecture . While anything after five years is pure speculation, we believe that if we make each piece of One Community easy to duplicate, free to access, and well-known, people will follow our lead and start to build their own prototype infrastructures and establish a sustainable world model founded on self-sufficient teacher/demonstration communities, villages, and cities .
